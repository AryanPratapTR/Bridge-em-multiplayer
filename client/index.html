How to Play Bridge-Em

Connect the starting letter to the ending letter with a valid English word!

🏆 Scoring

  • 4 letters: 5 points
  • 5 letters: 7 points
  • 6 letters: 9 points
  • 7 letters: 11 points
  • 8 letters: 13 points
  • 9+ letters: 15 points (Max!)

🎮 Game Modes

  • Limitless: Submit as many correct words as you want before time runs out!
  • Single Word (Speed): Everyone submits one word. The fastest players get large speed bonus multipliers!
  • Normal: Cutthroat! The round ends the moment the first person submits a valid word. Play fast!

📏 Levels

Difficulty auto-scales based on mathematically how many words actually exist for the pair. Prepare for Level 5!
